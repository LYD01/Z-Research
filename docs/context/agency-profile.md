# Agency Profile

> First draft based on how the agency was described at setup. Items marked **(assumption)** need confirming. Edit freely.

## Who we are

A one-person digital agency. We design, build and manage websites for small local businesses, mainly in the personal-services space (barbers, hairdressers, salons).

## Current offering

- Website design and build
- Ongoing website management and maintenance

## Direction

Move beyond "the website" to help clients with broader business processes, such as customer support, booking, communication and retention, by building or integrating the right tools. Ideas should become **repeatable services**, not one-off custom jobs.

## Constraints that shape every idea

| Constraint | Implication |
|------------|-------------|
| One person does sales, build, deploy, support | Favour low-maintenance, off-the-shelf, composable solutions. Avoid bespoke systems needing constant care |
| Support burden scales with client count | Anything we ship must be supportable at, say, 20+ clients without a team |
| Small budgets | Clients buy visible, simple value. Monthly costs must be clearly justified |
| Owners are time-poor and often non-technical | Minimal required effort from the owner. No dashboards they must check daily |
| Work happens in shops, on phones, in messaging apps | Meet customers and owners where they already are (e.g. messaging apps, phone calls, social) |
| Trust is the product | Handling customer data and automated communication carries reputational and legal risk |

## Typical client archetypes

- **Solo-operator appointment business**: one chair or one mobile practitioner, owner does everything
- **Small multi-staff salon/barber**: a handful of staff, shared bookings, a reception role that may be the owner
- **Appointment-based local service (adjacent)**: similar patterns in e.g. beauty, nails, massage, pet grooming **(assumption: relevant adjacent markets)**
