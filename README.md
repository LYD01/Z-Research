# Z-Research

A shared thinking space for a one-person digital agency that builds and manages websites for small businesses, such as barbers, hairdressers and salons.

## Why this exists

Client repos are the wrong place to think about a client's wider business. They anchor discussion to the website. This repo gives us room to explore the surrounding **business processes** (how customers find, book, pay, communicate with and return to a business) and to plan new features and services from there.

It is intentionally **client-agnostic**: ideas are framed for business archetypes so that anything worth building can become a repeatable service across many clients.

## What goes here

- **Ideas**: opportunities and concepts, at any stage of maturity
- **Research**: tools, vendors, technologies, markets, regulations
- **Process deep dives**: how a business process works today and where it hurts
- **Throwaway experiments**: small proofs of concept to answer a question

## What does not go here

- Client-repo implementation work
- Real client names, data, credentials or pricing
- Finished roadmaps for specific technologies (unless explicitly requested)

## Structure

| Path | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `AGENTS.md` | Context for AI agents |
| `.cursor/rules/` | Cursor rules (purpose, client-agnosticism, thinking approach, pricing, doc and research conventions) |
| `docs/context/` | Agency profile, business process map, evaluation framework, pricing and service models |
| `ideas/` | One file per idea |
| `research/` | Research notes and small experiments |
| `processes/` | Business process deep dives |
| `templates/` | Templates for new documents |

## Getting started

1. Skim `docs/context/` and correct anything that doesn't match reality. It is a first draft.
2. Copy a template from `templates/` into the right folder.
3. Work with the AI: describe a problem or process, and iterate.
